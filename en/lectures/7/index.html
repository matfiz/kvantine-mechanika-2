Dynamics: Stone’s theorem and the free wave packet

What this lecture does

Stone’s theorem says that the generator of every strongly continuous unitary group is self-adjoint. We prove this with vector integrals. The converse is proved for explicit diagonal and multiplication operators and sketched in general. For a free state with a rapidly decreasing transform, we compute the position variance in time.

The temperature of a cold gas is measured by letting it fly. In 1988 Lett, Watts, Westbrook, Phillips, Gould and Metcalf cooled sodium atoms with laser light, switched the light off, and timed the atoms’ arrival at a probe beam below. Dynamics

Evolution: the generator of every strongly continuous unitary group is self-adjoint (Theorem 7.8), and every self-adjoint HHH generates the group e−itH/ℏe^{-\ii tH/\hbar}e−itH/ℏ, proved for the explicit diagonal and multiplication operators (Theorem 7.4) and sketched in general (Theorem 7.5).
