The canonical pair: Weyl’s relation and Stone–von Neumann

We prove the Stone–von Neumann theorem: every irreducible Weyl pair of strongly continuous unitary groups is unitarily equivalent to the pair on the line. The proof builds a nonzero projection from Gaussian integrals. The box shows why the commutator on a dense domain is insufficient.

9.3 What the theorem decides

The second part of this lecture proves the theorem.

9.4 Von Neumann’s projection

The first part stated the Stone–von Neumann theorem and showed what it decides for a particle on the line. In this part we prove the theorem. The canonical pair

States: for a particle on the line with no further degree of freedom, every irreducible Weyl pair is the line’s, up to a unitary map (Theorem 9.5). So its state space is L2(R)\Ltwo(\R)L2(R).
