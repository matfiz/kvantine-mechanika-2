Lecture 10

The finite well: a spectrum in two parts

A finite well has both bound levels and a continuum. For a real bounded potential that vanishes outside a bounded interval and has at most finitely many jumps, we prove self-adjointness, simple negative levels and essential spectrum [0,∞)[0,\infty)[0,∞). We count the square-well levels, and prove that every such well with ∫V<0\int V<0∫V<0 binds.

Summary

  • A bounded potential keeps self-adjointness (Proposition 10.2), and for a self-adjoint operator whose means are bounded below, the bottom of the spectrum is the infimum of the means, which is the variational principle (Proposition 10.3).
  • The eigenvalue equation: the eigenvectors of HHH are the square-integrable, continuously differentiable solutions of (10.1) (Proposition 10.6); for a potential that vanishes outside a bounded interval, every level is negative and simple (Theorem 10.7).
  • The square well has ⌈2γ/π⌉\lceil2\gamma/\pi\rceil⌈2γ/π⌉ levels, alternately even and odd (Theorem 10.8).
