The finite well: a spectrum in two parts

What this lecture does

A finite well has both bound levels and a continuum. For a real bounded potential that vanishes outside a bounded interval and has at most finitely many jumps, we prove self-adjointness, simple negative levels and essential spectrum [0,∞)[0,\infty)[0,∞). We count the square-well levels, and prove that every such well with ∫V<0\int V<0∫V<0 binds.

Summary

  • A bounded potential keeps self-adjointness (Proposition 10.2), and for a self-adjoint operator whose means are bounded below, the bottom of the spectrum is the infimum of the means (Proposition 10.3).
