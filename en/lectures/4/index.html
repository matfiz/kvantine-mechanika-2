The free particle: observables without eigenvalues

What this lecture does

The Fourier transform builds the position, momentum and free energy on the line. These observables are self-adjoint and have no eigenvalues. For every unit vector in S(R)\mathcal S(\R)S(R), we prove uncertainty with equality exactly for the Gaussian packets.
