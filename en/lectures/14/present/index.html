Hydrogen: the Hamiltonian

We prove that the Hamiltonian of hydrogen is self-adjoint on the domain of the free energy. Hydrogen is stable: no state has an energy mean below −Ry-\mathrm{Ry}−Ry, and −Ry-\mathrm{Ry}−Ry is an eigenvalue. The spectrum contains [0,∞)[0,\infty)[0,∞). Below zero it is discrete, and every eigenvalue is negative. Lecture 15 finds these eigenvalues.
